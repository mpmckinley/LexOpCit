Op. Cit.

an abbreviation of the Latin phrase opus citatum or opere citato, meaning the work cited or in the cited work.

Our site is an excercise in love (and figuring out how to code a webpage). We are passionate about and appreciative for the thought, discussion, and ideas Lex and his guests have brought to the masses through the Lex Fridman Podcast.

Where we try to capture all meaningful content mentioned or discussed at any length during a podcast - having a passion for reading, our focus is books. All else, including film, papers, essays, podcasts, videos, music, TV, and games we have attempted to compile as ephemera.
